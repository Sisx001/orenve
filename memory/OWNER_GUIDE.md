# Welcome to the ORYNVE owner studio

1. Open `/admin` and sign in with your generated credentials.
2. **Products**: open a piece to edit its information, photography, stock, sizes, details and search metadata. Product visibility controls take effect at the next publish.
3. **Media library**: upload your own photos/video. Use **Choose from library** in a product or homepage image field.
4. **Homepage**: edit the hero; open individual sections to edit, enable, reorder, duplicate or remove them.
5. **Contact channels**: add your WhatsApp number with country code. Add your email, support details and social links as desired.
6. **Currencies**: maintain indicative rates from 1 BDT. The website explains that final payment is agreed in BDT.
7. Click **Save draft**. The public website does not change.
8. Click **Preview** to inspect desktop, tablet and mobile views or compare with the live store. Preview links expire after two hours.
9. Click **Publish**, then confirm. Your changes become public together.
10. **Revision history** lets you restore an earlier live version to a draft before republishing.

## Important
- A prepared WhatsApp order is an inquiry, not a confirmed sale. Customers review and send the message; your team confirms availability, shipping and payment.
- Without a business WhatsApp number, customers can copy their summary and use the contact form, but cannot open your business conversation.
- Contact messages and newsletter signups appear under **Inquiries & community**. No automated email campaign/delivery service is connected.
- Review initial product photography, materials, fit charts, stock and policies against your real inventory before accepting orders.
- Uploaded files are stored persistently. Used assets cannot be deleted until their references are removed and those changes are published.
- There are no customer accounts. Keep your studio credentials private.
